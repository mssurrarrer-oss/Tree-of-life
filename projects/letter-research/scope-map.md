# Scope Map

## Objective
Create a rigorous, evidence-grounded framework for interpreting letter-level logic across language, symbolic systems, and domain terminology.

## In-scope domains
- Language and orthography
- Symbol interpretation
- Scientific terminology mapping
- Medical terminology mapping
- Religious text interpretation (comparative and evidence-cited)

## Benefits to measure
- Consistency of interpretation across models
- Reduction in ambiguity for key terms
- Better retrieval and report generation quality
- Faster onboarding of new evidence

## Risks to manage
- Over-generalization from weak evidence
- Confirmation bias
- Cross-domain claim leakage
- Misclassification of symbolic meaning

## Success criteria
- Every major claim has traceable sources
- Evidence has confidence and counterexample handling
- Reports are reproducible across model runs
- Weekly delta shows net increase in confirmed claims
