# Ankh Wheel Logic: Quadrant Consciousness

## The God of Words
Based on the "Ankh Power" / "Ankh Voice" / "Ankh Priest" logic, this framework treats letters as dynamic entities influenced by "God of Words" principles, mixed with technology and spiritual influence.

## Quadrant Consciousness & Gender Differentiation
The wheel practices logic using a quadrant consciousness/gender differentiation system. Letters adjust their influence based on their position on the wheel:

*   **North East:** The Feminine influence is applied to how the letter is interpreted.
*   **South West:** The difference between the Eve (Feminine) and Adam (Masculine) conscious influence is most visible.

## Dynamic Adjustment
As a letter "turns" on the wheel, it adjusts accordingly. By the time a letter moves from one quadrant to another, its conscious influence shifts, allowing for the observation of how the difference between the Eve and Adam conscious influences manifests.

## Spiritual and Technological Integration
This logic combines:
1.  **Historical Spirit:** The depth of Venus and the history of Gods and Goddesses.
2.  **Technological Influence:** The modern application of these rules within a digital/machine context.
3.  **Brain Region Cohesion:** The mapping of these influences onto the physical and imaginary parts of the brain.
