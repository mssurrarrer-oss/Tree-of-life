# Observations on K, V, Y, F, E, I, and Related Letters

**Source author:** Michael Surrarrer
**Date:** 2026-07-26
**Status:** provisional

## V
- Vocative / action shape.
- Two converging lines doing both genders together in harmonious vocation.
- Position inside a word determines which side supplies the initiating world and which side supplies the confirming participle.

## K
- As in "King": starts with an `l`/`I` vertical anchor and contains a horizontal `V` in the middle.
- The K vocates the breadth of masculine and feminine consciousness, not just both sides together.
- It connects from the equator between both genders and fuses the E for both genders.
- Each gender has its own E: boy and eve are both already Adam-and-Eve, and every letter includes both genders' places.
- Suggests rulership as masculine anchoring with reach into both genders' territories.
- Compare King (K + I + N + G) with Queen (Q + U + double E + N): the two Es mark the feminine world as attached to each gender's consciousness.

## F and E relationship
- The word Female compared to Eve shows that F and E share the top and middle lines.
- The E adds a lower line, indicating a lower self in harmony with the Female, or the Boy producing an effect/act.
- The extra line proceeds from the Boy's consciousness in harmony with the third-person middle line and the first-person top line.
- "First" and "Last" likely map to first-person versus last-person perspective. "Last person" is not a modern dictionary term but appears biblically: the feminine world a Boy is attached to, or an Adam doing an act.
- Female is a feminine act proceeding from Eve, the Boy/world the Adam sees, and the inclusive third-person midst.

## I
- The little `i` is a scheduled act to make communion across the gender-consciousness divide.
- The top of the base of the lowercase `i` sits at the conscious equator, a third-person perspective inclusive of both genders.
- The bottom of the stem is from the Boy/Adam's consciousness.
- The dot is in the midst of the feminine conscious zone.
- The `ih` sound creates and excites the connection that takes both genders to accomplish.
- Compare brain-region words: Amygdala, Hypothalamus, Hippocampus versus Cerebellum. Both genders participate in both orientations; a female orients toward the top-right area, a male toward the lower-left.

## Y
- Like a V with a little `l` or `i` as a leg.
- "Aye" contains A + E together and apart: A and E together say "I".
- Y means "as one and as two" — unity that preserves distinction.
- The leg adds the lifeline / intent stream to the vocative convergence.

## A/E duality
- A + E together can be read as "I" — the self as the union of Adam-anchor and Eve-insertion.
- This appears in words and sounds where identity is formed by both gendered participles.

## El / Elohim note
- "El" can indicate the feminine continent (Spanish/Eurasian landmass in the geographic schema).
- "Elohim" doubles the El, showing the feminine continent from both masculine and feminine perspectives joined together.
- This connects to words like King (divine feminine specialization) and to biblical editions such as the 1611 KJV.
