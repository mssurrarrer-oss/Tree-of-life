# Research Report Specification

## Audience variants
- Variant A: technical evidence brief
- Variant B: narrative interpretation brief
- Variant C: executive scope and risk summary

## Required sections
1. Scope and assumptions
2. Claim inventory summary
3. Confirmed claims with citations
4. Provisional claims and open questions
5. Risks and mitigations
6. Recommended next experiments

## Citation policy
- Every non-trivial claim must cite at least one evidence record.
- Include model/software provenance for generated analysis text.
- Distinguish direct evidence from inferred reasoning.

## Quality gates
- No uncited confirmed claims
- Counterexample review complete for new high-impact claims
- At least one independent model cross-check on critical claims
