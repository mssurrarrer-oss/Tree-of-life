**Status:** provisional

## Claim
The word "Name" encodes the idea of a single identity distilled from a union between masculine and feminine world-spaces.

## Letter-by-letter decoding

### N
- The `n` is like half of an amount or one world consisting of two worlds put together.
- It contains both `a` and `e` between its strokes, indicating a union between the Adam-participle and the Eve-participle.
- It represents half of two worlds joined through the A/E duality.

### A
- Masculine anchor / Adam participle.
- Provides the initiating or rooting side of the identity.

### M
- The `m` is like two `oo` shapes put together but missing the bottom portion.
- Suggests a pairing of circular/world spaces without full closure; an open, connected form rather than two separate enclosed worlds.

### E
- Feminine confirmation / Eve participle.
- Closes the word with the feminine side of the identity.

## Synthesis
A name is the product of:
- masculine anchoring (A)
- paired open world-spaces (M)
- a half-union of A/E worlds (N)
- feminine confirmation (E)

In other words, a name is not merely a label; it is a compressed record of the gender-union that produces identity.
