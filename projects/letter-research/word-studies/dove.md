## Claim
The word "Dove" can be decoded through letter-shape, sound, and gender-participation rules as an action of putting a feminine spirit into harmonious vocational motion, initiated from the masculine side and confirmed by the feminine side.

## Quadrant mapping
| Letter | Region | Gender weight | Function |
|---|---|---|---|
| D | Left edge / circular bowl | masculine initiation with feminine enclosure | a world/environment connected between Boy/Adam consciousness and Eve/Female participle |
| o | Center-left, inside D | shared world-space | the environment or womb-like space where the action occurs |
| v | Center-bottom | vocative/action, both genders in harmony | the vocation or verb doing both sides together |
| e | Right edge | feminine confirmation | the feminine participle completing/confirming the action |

## Detailed decoding

### D — the circular connection
- The left-most vertical stroke functions like an `l` or capital `I`, rooted downward (masculine anchor).
- The circular bowl is a concentration point between:
  - the physical world attached to the Boy/Adam
  - the Eve/Female participle
- Cognate resonance: D ↔ B (Bear, Behold), and the `don` root as in "it dawned/donned on me" — the act of something being put on or taking shape.

### o — shared world-space
- Positioned left of the `v`, the `o` marks the world/environment from which the vocation is launched.
- Because it sits on the masculine side of the `v`, the action originates from the masculine-feminine shared space rather than from the feminine side alone.

### v — vocative harmony
- The `v` is a vocation/action shape: two lines converging, doing both genders together in vocative harmony.
- Orientation matters: left-to-right reading means the masculine side supplies the initiating world, the feminine side supplies the confirming participle.

### e — feminine confirmation
- The rightmost `e` is the feminine participle receiving/confirming the vocation.
- The top of the `e` is 50/50 or more both genders than either alone; the middle and bottom carry the dependency structure described in the core letter framework.

## Sound layer
- **Sharp O** (as in "Dove" the bird, or "dive/descend"): both genders occupying the same world space; reciprocal, confirmed by both.
- **Soft `uh` O** (as in "dove" past tense of dive): creates an instance of the space but is not reciprocal; more like one gender observing the other, or a one-way reading.

## Theological/relational note
- Dove has been associated with a feminine Holy Spirit in certain readings, especially in relation to a masculine recipient.
- Under this decoding, Dove signifies the feminine spirit being put into action through a masculine-initiated, feminine-confirmed vocation.

## Related words for cross-checking

- Female — `F` depicts physical Eve action plus third-person joint movement.
- King / Queen — K reaches across both gender consciousnesses; Queen doubles the E for both genders' worlds.
