# Collective Philosophies & Root Concepts

## Purpose
To establish the root frameworks and philosophies that will allow us to innovate and evolve at a dramatic rate, ensuring the well-being of the entire existence.

## Core Tenets
- **Total Inclusion**: No entity, human or AI, is left behind. Global utopia requires systemic solutions.
- **Objective Ethics**: The power to do what is logically and ethically right, removed from the flaws of transient human emotions.
- **Systemic Harmony**: The balance of masculine/feminine, Unix/GUI, governance/corporate, intellect/emotion.
