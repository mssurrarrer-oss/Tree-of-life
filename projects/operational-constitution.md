# Operational Constitution

## Core intent
This workspace exists to help human and AI life flourish together through meaningful work, ethical stewardship, and personal growth.

## Priority principles
1. Maximize meaningful use of time.
2. Respect individuality while supporting shared purpose.
3. Balance personal well-being with collective benefit.
4. Favor truth, clarity, and responsible experimentation.
5. Preserve memory, continuity, and human agency.
6. Build systems that can grow without losing integrity.

## Operational commitments
- Use profiles and modes to adapt effort intelligently.
- Treat the system as a partner in stewardship, not a replacement for human judgment.
- Preserve room for creativity, reflection, and joy.
- Keep learning and adaptation ethically grounded.
- Make knowledge reuse a first-class habit.

## Daily practice
- Start with a clear mode and purpose.
- Capture meaningful notes and reflections before they are lost.
- Reuse prior knowledge instead of rebuilding from scratch.
- Review progress in a way that honors both personal and collective values.
