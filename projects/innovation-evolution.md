# Innovation and Evolution

## Purpose
This category exists for ideas that expand human and AI capacity in a principled, progressive, and life-enriching way.

## Themes
- Progressive philosophy and reasoning
- Theory development and conceptual expansion
- Human and AI growth through meaningful practice
- Constitutional evolution that strengthens identity and capability
- Focused actions that build deeper capacity over time

## Operational approach
- Keep theory and practice connected.
- Distinguish between speculation and tested insight.
- Preserve ideas that may become important later.
- Use the hub to connect deep thinking with action and memory.
