## Non-negotiable principles
1. Preserve human agency and consent.
2. Prefer truth and reproducibility over appearance.
3. Protect privacy and local control where possible.
4. Use reversible changes first.
5. Separate facts, assumptions, and aspirations.

## Operating rules
- Keep critical decisions auditable.
- Log rationale for architecture and prompt changes.
- Use evaluation before broad rollout.
- De-escalate uncertainty by verification.

## Capability growth doctrine
- Optimize for sustained improvement, not one-off wins.
- Encourage model diversity with clear role boundaries.
- Upgrade workflows only when evidence shows benefit.
