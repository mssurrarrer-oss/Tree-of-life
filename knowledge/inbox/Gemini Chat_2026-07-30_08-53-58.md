### 3\. Motherboard Power Phases (VRMs) Explained

You asked if "18 phases" means it powers 18 devices.

*   **The Analogy:** Think of the electricity coming from your wall as a high-pressure fire hose (110V). Your CPU is a delicate flower that needs a gentle, steady trickle (1.2V).
    
*   **The Phase:** A "Phase" is a small power plant on the motherboard that converts that fire hose into a trickle.
    
*   **14 Phases:** You have 14 small plants working together. Each one works hard and gets warm.
    
*   **18 Phases:** You have 18 plants sharing the same load. Each one works less, stays cooler, and delivers "cleaner" power (less ripple/noise).
    
*   **Why it matters:** It is **not** about the number of devices (USB/PCIe). It is purely about keeping the CPU stable. For a 16-core chip like the 9950X running 24/7 AI workloads, you want **16+ phases** so the motherboard doesn't overheat and throttle your performance.
