**The PCIe Lane Bottleneck (AtomMan X7-Ti)** You mentioned having an M.2 2280 slot for PCIe devices and hoping that leaves room for 16 lanes. It is critical to clarify that an standard M.2 2280 slot is physically and electrically limited to a maximum of 4 PCIe lanes (specifically PCIe 4.0 x4 on the AtomMan X7-Ti) `. The system's dedicated OCuLink port is also strictly limited to 4 PCIe lanes` .

Because the AtomMan X7-Ti utilizes a mobile-class Core Ultra 9 185H processor inside a mini-PC chassis \`\`, it simply does not have the extensive PCIe lane availability of a desktop or server processor. You do not have a full x16 slot to split or bifurcate between multiple devices.

If you connect an external GPU—even an x8 card like the RTX 5060 Ti—via an M.2 adapter or the OCuLink port, it will be forced to run at x4 bandwidth. This significantly throttles the data transfer speeds between your system memory and the GPU. While x4 bandwidth is often acceptable for gaming, it creates a severe bottleneck for AI inference and model training, which require moving massive amounts of tensor data back and forth. To utilize 16 or more lanes for multiple AI accelerators, you will need to rely on the dedicated server architecture (like the AMD EPYC platform) discussed in the core research plan.
