# Starter Knowledge Notes

## Language and symbol work
The goal is to build a durable local system for understanding letters, sounds, and meanings in a consistent and testable way.

## VR and AR work
The Meta Quest 3 and XR workflow should be explored as a creative environment for immersive AI experiences.

## Local AI principles
Use local-first workflows where possible, preserve privacy, and keep a strong ethical foundation.

## Memory and retrieval
A local knowledge base should make it possible to rediscover prior work without rebuilding from scratch.
