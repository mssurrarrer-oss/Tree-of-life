# AI Hub

This folder is a simple local launchpad for your AI work.

## Structure
- notes/ — ideas, meeting notes, and summaries
- prompts/ — reusable prompt templates
- projects/ — experiments, builds, and research folders

## Use it
1. Open index.html in your browser.
2. Save notes and tasks locally in your browser.
3. Add your own files under the folders above as your workflow grows.
